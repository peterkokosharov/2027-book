# Note

> *All houses, persons and towns in this book are invented. The public numbers — prices, rounds, dates, counts that
> can be checked — are not; the small numbers inside invented scenes are the scenes' own. Where the record ends — late
> in 2026 — the Ledger goes on writing, and from that line onward its entries are forecasts: they describe no real
> person's acts. It says where it crosses.*
